Recognition

for eighteen years.

AROUND MANUNGKOT

Places worth
taking the road for.

Stay in Manungkot and take your time exploring the hills, villages, viewpoints and towns that surround us.

Cloud Views

Watch the clouds drift through the hills of Manungkot, filling the valleys with mist and changing the landscape from one moment to the next.

Bandipur

Wander through the charming hilltop town of Bandipur, known for its traditional Newari architecture, stone streets, mountain views and relaxed atmosphere.

Pokhara

Spend a day discovering Pokhara — from the shores of Phewa Lake and lakeside cafés to mountain views, local markets and the city's many outdoor experiences.

Hillside Walks

Follow quiet paths through the surrounding hills and countryside, passing terraces, forests and small settlements along the way.

Sunrise & Sunset

Start the morning with changing light across the hills and return in the evening as the landscape turns golden beneath the open Himalayan sky.

Local Life

Meet the people, discover local traditions and experience the slower side of life in the villages and hills around Manungkot.
